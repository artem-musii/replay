# REPLAY judging matrix

Last reconciled with current local source/tests and separately scoped deployed evidence: **2026-08-29**. The [official challenge page](https://openai.com/webmcp-challenge/) and [rules](https://webmcp.devpost.com/rules) define four equally weighted criteria, with WebMCP Leverage first in tie-breaking, and a **September 3, 2026 at 1:00 PM PDT** deadline. The final submission must provide a functioning live WebMCP URL, public source/instructions/license, English description, free judge access, and a public YouTube demo under three minutes with audio and actual WebMCP use. This matrix links claims to source, tests, public artifact/browser evidence, and remaining external proof without conflating those categories.

## Judge takeaway

| Criterion             | What REPLAY proves in the first viewing                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WebMCP Leverage       | A screenshot shows vehicles; typed Site Tools expose their branch-owned timed points, evidence relationships, locks, and current version, then act through the same commands as the UI.   |
| Execution             | One synthetic case moves from calibrated authored motion through provenance/completeness review to a citation-bound report, with every state and consequential decision visible.          |
| Potential Impact      | A driver and claims-intake reviewer turn fragmented incident material into a cited neutral record while evidence, memory, dispute, uncertainty, attestation, and inference stay distinct. |
| Creativity & Ambition | A scene and timeline become a shared human-agent black box: the agent reads, validates, proposes, and drafts; the human attests, decides, and finalizes.                                  |

**Operating contract:** Agent: read, validate, propose, draft. Reviewer: attest, decide, finalize.

Status vocabulary:

- **Implemented in current source:** present in current source/tests, without implying deployment, a native client, or a manual live journey.
- **Deployed + independently payload-verified:** present in commit `2855f0bc50da2916128b2278a46f0d0a8a4e2bbd`, passed CI, and matched its Pages artifact in the independent 2026-08-29 cache-busted public-file comparison.
- **Historical public proof:** verified for superseded commit `00688d8a` or the older `f980d28` baseline; retained as evidence but not attributed to the current release.
- **Specified; run pending:** an eval/check artifact exists without a recorded result.
